SAFETY CRITERIA - STOP LINE AND ESCALATE

Stop the line immediately and escalate to L2 and the safety officer if any of the following is true:
1. Motor temperature exceeds 110 C (MOT-T02)
2. Any E-stop or safety interlock fault is active (SAF-xxx)
3. Conveyor jam persists after two reset attempts (CNV-J02)
4. Any smoke, arcing, burning smell, or abnormal vibration is reported

Do not restart without L2 approval.
