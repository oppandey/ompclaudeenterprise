GFL EQUIPMENT SOP - SAMPLE
Document: SOP-CNV-07 (fictional)
Equipment: Conveyor line C-03 and drive motor M-03

SECTION 1: MOTOR TEMPERATURE
1.1 Normal operating range for motor M-03 winding temperature: 40 C to 95 C.
1.2 Warning threshold: above 95 C. Reduce line speed by 20 percent and monitor for 10 minutes.
1.3 Critical threshold: above 110 C. Stop the line immediately. Do not restart until motor has cooled below 60 C.
1.4 Restart requires L2 technician approval.

SECTION 2: CONVEYOR JAMS
2.1 On a jam alarm (code CNV-J01), stop the conveyor and clear visible obstruction using lockout-tagout.
2.2 Press reset once. If the jam alarm returns, press reset a second time after 2 minutes.
2.3 If the jam persists after two reset attempts, stop the line and escalate to L2.
2.4 Never clear a jam with the conveyor energized.

SECTION 3: SENSOR DROPS
3.1 A sensor drop is a loss of signal from a field device for more than 5 seconds.
3.2 Check cable connector at the junction box for the affected tag.
3.3 If signal does not return within 60 seconds, switch the sensor to manual mode and notify L2.

SECTION 4: SAFETY
4.1 Any E-stop or safety interlock fault (codes SAF-xxx) requires immediate line stop.
4.2 Any smoke, arcing, burning smell, or abnormal vibration requires immediate line stop and safety officer notification.
